Agristack Captcha Solver Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how Agristack Captcha Solver, published by Texter Computer
(“the extension”), handles information when you use it.

Information the extension handles

The extension runs on agristack.gov.in and its subdomains. It detects responses
to the site's CAPTCHA request and sends the response payload to the solver
service at https://textercompter.store/agri/captcha_solver.php. The payload is
the data returned by that CAPTCHA request; it may contain the CAPTCHA image or
other fields included in the response. The extension sends the payload so the
service can return a CAPTCHA solution.

The extension stores the following in Chrome local storage on your device:

- The total number of CAPTCHAs solved and the number solved today.
- The date used for the daily count.
- The most recently solved CAPTCHA's image, answer, and time.

This information remains in the browser until you clear the extension's data or
remove the extension. The extension does not sync this information through a
developer account.

Solver service and retention

The CAPTCHA payload is transmitted to the solver service over HTTPS. The
extension's code does not establish whether the service stores submitted
payloads, how long any server-side information is retained, or when it is
deleted. Before publication, the developer should add the service's actual
retention and deletion practices here and confirm that they match this policy.
The service may also receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP
address, as part of handling the request.

Other sharing and use

The extension does not include analytics code. Its popup contains an optional
link to another Texter Computer extension in the Chrome Web Store; clicking the
link opens that store listing.

Your choices

You can stop this processing by disabling or removing the extension. You can
remove the extension to clear its locally stored counts and last CAPTCHA.
Removing the extension does not necessarily delete information already sent to
the solver service; contact the service operator about server-side deletion
requests.

Children

The extension is intended for use with the Agristack website and is not
designed specifically for children.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, this policy will be updated. The
effective date above indicates when it was last revised.
